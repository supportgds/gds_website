How a month runs

The same close process, every month

You always know where your books are. Each month follows the same four stages, and we agree your reporting date with you upfront.

Step 1Collect

Pull in the month

Bank feeds, statements, receipts and payroll reports come in. We chase anything missing.

Step 2Reconcile

Match every account

Bank, card, loan and clearing accounts are reconciled to the cent and uncoded items cleared.

Step 3Adjust

Post the close entries

Accruals, prepayments, depreciation and payroll journals. A second reviewer checks the work.

Step 4Report

Deliver the numbers

Financial statements and a plain-English summary land in your inbox, with time to talk them through.

Our story

From freelance desks to a partnership

All three partners studied accountancy at Holy Angel University. Ann and Katrizia started working together as freelance accountants on Upwork in 2015. As their client list grew, they invited Hazel, who was working overseas in Dubai at the time, to join them.

In 2018 the three made it official as Galban, Dimacje & Sicat Accounting Firm. We have grown from three partners to a team of 10, and have served more than 10 clients across the United States and Australia.

We exist to give business owners accurate information they can run their business on, and to build a team where every person's skills are put to full use.

Questions

Before you get in touch

Which accounting software do you use?

We work in your existing QuickBooks Online or Xero file. If you are not on cloud software yet, we can set it up and migrate your history.

My books are behind. Can you catch them up?

Yes. We quote a one-off clean-up to bring the books current, then move you onto our monthly close process.

How is the fee set?

After a free review of your books we agree a fixed monthly fee based on transaction volume, accounts and the services you choose.

How do you keep my data secure?

We work inside your own systems with individual logins you control, and access can be revoked at any time. Nothing is kept on personal devices.

Do you file taxes?

We prepare tax-ready books and work alongside your local CPA or tax adviser, who handles filings in your country.

What does a dedicated accountant mean?

One named person works on your account as part of your team, with a reviewer checking their close each month and cover during leave.
